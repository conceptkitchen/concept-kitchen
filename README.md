# The Concept Kitchen 🍳

**"Anybody can cook."**

AI/tech literacy brand making technology accessible to people who feel intimidated by it.

## What's Here

- `index.html` - Brand identity showcase (colors, typography, mockups)
- `logos.html` - Logo explorations (6 concepts)
- `lockups.html` - Refined logo lockups with wordmarks

## Brand Overview

- **Philosophy:** You don't need formal training or credentials to get results with AI
- **Voice:** Cook, not a classically trained chef
- **Target:** Non-technical people intimidated by AI/tech
