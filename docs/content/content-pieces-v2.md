# The Concept Kitchen — 25 Content Pieces (v2)

Based on: Northbound Labs framework, 3 personas (Marcus, Diana, James), research-backed pain points, "Rebels" positioning, cooking metaphor.

---

## Content Mix Target
- **TOFU (40%)**: Identity Shift, Fear Transformation — viral hooks, emotional connection
- **MOFU (40%)**: Quick Wins, Time Reclaimed, Role-Specific — practical value, nurture
- **BOFU (20%)**: Proof, results, lead magnet CTAs — conversion
