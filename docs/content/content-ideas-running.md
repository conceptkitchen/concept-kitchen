### IDEA #001: Expert AI Users Know When AI is Lying

**Raw Idea:**
What separates an expert AI user is they know when the AI is lying. Don't put all your trust in AI. Trust yourself and verify.

**Context:**
Most beginners think AI is always right. They copy-paste outputs without checking. Experts know AI hallucinates — makes up facts, cites fake sources, gives confident wrong answers. The skill isn't blind trust, it's knowing when to verify. This positions RJ as someone who teaches REAL AI skills, not hype.

**Content Summary:**
- Hook: "The #1 thing that separates AI beginners from experts"
- Body: It's not prompting. It's not knowing all the tools. It's knowing when AI is WRONG.
- AI lies. Confidently. It makes up facts, fake sources, wrong numbers.
- Beginners copy-paste. Experts verify.
- The skill: Trust yourself first. Use AI as a draft, not a final answer.
- CTA: "What's the worst AI mistake you've caught?"

**Format Options:**
- Talking head reel (60 sec) — direct, personal
- Carousel (5 slides) — educational breakdown
- Static post with strong hook — quick hit

**Pillar:** Quick Wins / Identity Shift (hybrid — practical skill + "become an expert")

**Status:** Idea

### IDEA #002: Triggers and Shortcuts in AI Instructions

**Raw Idea:**
Add triggers to instructions so when you say keywords or shortcuts (like hashtags or codes), AI knows what to do. Example: #ideas to signal idea dumping and organize info a specific way.

**Context:**
Power users create their own "command language" with AI. Instead of explaining what you want every time, you set up triggers in Custom Instructions. When you type a keyword, AI knows exactly how to respond. This is a level-up from basic prompting — it's building your own AI workflow system.

**Content Summary:**
- Hook: "Turn AI into YOUR assistant with one trick"
- Body: Most people start from scratch every chat. Waste of time.
- Set up TRIGGERS in your instructions. Keywords that tell AI what mode to be in.
- Examples:
  - "#ideas" = I'm brainstorming, capture everything loosely
  - "#draft" = Give me a rough first pass, don't overthink
  - "#final" = Polish this, make it clean
  - "#roast" = Give me honest critical feedback
- You're not just using AI — you're programming it to work YOUR way.
- CTA: "What triggers would you set up? Drop them below."

**Format Options:**
- Carousel (6-7 slides) — show the trigger examples visually
- Reel with screen recording — demonstrate it live
- Thread/caption with list — text-heavy, saveable

**Pillar:** Quick Wins / Role-Specific (power user technique)

**Status:** Idea
