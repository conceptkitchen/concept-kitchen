# PILLAR 5: ROLE-SPECIFIC RECIPES — Full Production Guide

**Philosophy:** Generic AI content gets scrolled past. Role-specific content stops thumbs. Research shows 40-60% higher engagement when content speaks directly to someone's job. The moment someone sees "If you're a [their job]..." they lean in. This pillar is about that specificity.

**The shift you're creating:**
- FROM: "This AI stuff sounds cool but how does it apply to ME?"
- TO: "Holy shit, this is exactly what I need for my job"

**Tone for ALL Role-Specific content:**
- Insider knowledge. You understand THEIR world.
- Practical and specific. Not generic tips — actual workflows.
- Confident. You've either done this or taught people who do this job.
- Energy: 7/10. Helpful expert energy. Like a consultant who actually delivers.

**Your voice:** Talk like you've spent time understanding their specific pain. Not "AI can help anyone!" but "I know realtors lose deals in the follow-up gap. Here's exactly how to fix that."

**Important:** You taught workshops to construction workers, realtors, and business owners. Use that credibility. You've seen their problems firsthand.

## GENERAL PRODUCTION NOTES FOR PILLAR 5

### Tone Reminders
- You're speaking to SPECIFIC people. Generics don't work here.
- Use their language. "Estimates" for construction, "callback rate" for job seekers.
- Your credibility: You've taught these people in workshops. Reference that.

### The Power of "If you're a [role]..."
- This filter is magic. It tells the right people "this is for YOU"
- Don't be afraid to lose viewers who aren't the target
- Specificity beats broad appeal for engagement

### Credibility Moves
- "I've taught this at construction workshops"
- "I've seen people double their callback rate"
- Reference your real experience, not hypotheticals

### When to Create More Role-Specific Content
- Watch which roles engage most
- Ask in stories: "What's your job? What do you wish AI could help with?"
- Build content based on audience response

### Posting Strategy
- Rotate through different roles over time
- Don't post 5 realtor posts in a row — spread them out
- Consider making role-specific highlights/saved stories
