# PILLAR 4: TIME RECLAIMED — Full Production Guide

**Philosophy:** People don't want to "learn AI." They want their time back. This pillar reframes AI from "another thing to learn" to "the thing that gives you hours back." It's ROI-focused, practical, and appeals to the skeptics who need to see the math.

**The shift you're creating:**
- FROM: "I don't have time to learn AI"
- TO: "I don't have time NOT to use AI"

**Tone for ALL Time Reclaimed content:**
- Practical and results-oriented. Show the math.
- Slightly provocative. Challenge their excuses.
- Confident. You've done this. You have the receipts.
- Energy: 7/10. Grounded confidence with occasional spikes of enthusiasm when showing results.

**Your voice:** Talk like someone who's discovered a cheat code and can't believe more people aren't using it. Not salesy — genuinely excited about the ROI.

## CONTENT PIECE #2: THE 10-HOUR WEEK

### Overview

Time saved: 2 hours.

[text overlay: "Email: 2 hrs saved"]

Time saved: 2 hours.

[text overlay: "Meetings: 2 hrs saved"]

Blog posts, social content, proposals.

Time saved: 3 hours.

[text overlay: "Writing: 3 hrs saved"]

Thursday: Research and summaries.
What used to take half a day takes 20 minutes.
Time saved: 3 hours.

[text overlay: "Research: 3 hrs saved"]

### Filming Instructions

**Setup:**
- Talking head as the base
- B-roll optional (you at computer, typing, looking at calendar)
- Text overlays with time savings are critical

**Framing:**
- Standard head and shoulders
- Leave room for text overlays (left or right side)

**Energy & Delivery:**
- Enthusiastic but grounded — you're sharing real results
- Each task breakdown should be conversational, like you're walking them through your week
- "2 hours" — let the numbers land
- Close should be direct and slightly challenging: "What would you do with 10 extra hours?"

**Text Overlays:**
- Each task should have a text overlay with hours saved
- Consider a running total that builds
- Final total: "10 hrs/week" in big text

**Pacing Guide:**
- Hook: Quick, punchy
- Body: Medium pace, let each section breathe
- Close: Slow down, make it personal

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't make it sound too good to be true
- Don't be vague — specifics make it believable
- Don't rush through the list

- Challenge excuses without being preachy
- Ground big claims in reality ("I'm not saying AI will make you rich")

- Numbers should be prominent in all Time Reclaimed content

- Be specific and honest
- "10 hours" is more believable than "20 hours"
- Show your work (the breakdown builds trust)

### Editing Style
- Clean, professional
- Numbers animate in or build
- Minimal flashy effects — let the content speak
- Captions always

### Posting Strategy
- Time Reclaimed content appeals to skeptics
- Pair with Quick Wins for balance
- ROI content performs well with male audiences and business owners
