# PILLAR 3: QUICK WINS — Full Production Guide

**Philosophy:** 52% of people never start courses they sign up for. Tutorial paralysis is real. The cure isn't more information — it's ONE small win. Quick Wins content gives them something they can DO right now, proves they're capable, and builds momentum.

**The shift you're creating:**
- FROM: "I don't know where to start"
- TO: "Holy shit, I just did that"

**Tone for ALL Quick Wins content:**
- Practical and energetic. You're in the kitchen cooking WITH them.
- Clear and direct. No fluff. Here's what to do.
- Encouraging but not hand-holdy. They're capable — treat them like it.
- Energy: 8/10. This is action content. More energy than Identity Shift or Fear Transformation.

**Your voice:** Talk like a friend showing you a cool trick. "Dude, check this out — you can do this in like 2 minutes."

## CONTENT PIECE #1: THE CHATGPT FIX

### Overview
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Format** | Reel (screen share + voiceover OR talking head + text overlay) |
| **Length** | 30-45 seconds |
| **Funnel Stage** | MOFU (Nurture) |
| **Target Persona** | All |
| **Goal** | Give them ONE fix they can use immediately. Build confidence. |

### The Hook (First 3 Seconds)
**What you say:** "Most people use ChatGPT wrong. Here's the one-line fix."

**How to deliver it:**
- Direct, confident. You know something they don't (yet).
- "Most people" — not accusatory, just factual
- "One-line fix" — creates curiosity. Just one line? I can do that.

### The Script (Full)

```
[HOOK — 0:00-0:03]
"Most people use ChatGPT wrong. Here's the one-line fix."

[BODY — 0:03-0:30]
Here's what most prompts look like:

[show on screen or text overlay]
"Write me an email to my boss."

And you get back... generic garbage.

Here's the fix:

[show on screen]
"You are a professional executive assistant. 
I need a concise email to my boss requesting time off next Friday. 
Keep it professional but warm. 
Here's the context: [add your situation]."

Same tool. 10x better output.

The difference? 
You gave it a role, a specific task, and context.

[CLOSE — 0:30-0:40]
That's it. That's the fix.

Try it right now. Screenshot this prompt.

Come back and tell me how it went.
```

### Filming Instructions

**Option A: Screen Recording**
- Record your screen showing ChatGPT
- Type the bad prompt, show the meh response
- Type the good prompt, show the better response
- Voiceover throughout
- Face cam in corner optional

**Option B: Talking Head + Text Overlay**
- Talk direct to camera
- Text overlays show the prompts
- Bad prompt in red or struck through
- Good prompt in green or highlighted

**Setup (if talking head):**
- Standard vertical setup
- Slightly faster energy than previous pillars
- This is practical, action-oriented content

**Framing:**
- If screen share: Full screen with optional face cam bubble
- If talking head: Standard head/shoulders, leave room for text overlays

**Energy & Delivery:**
- More animated than Identity Shift or Fear Transformation
- You're excited to share this trick
- "Generic garbage" — say it like you're slightly annoyed at bad outputs
- The reveal of the fix should feel like "here's the secret"
- Close is encouraging and action-oriented

**Pacing Guide:**
- Hook: Quick, punchy
- Bad example: Slightly dismissive
- Fix reveal: Slow down slightly, let them read/absorb
- Close: Energetic, call to action

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't over-explain. Keep it tight.
- Don't use jargon ("prompt engineering"). Just show them.
- Don't make it seem complicated. It's ONE fix.

```
Try it right now:

"You are a professional executive assistant. I need a concise email to my boss requesting time off next Friday. Keep it professional but warm. Here's my situation: [your context]."
```

### Overview

| **Length** | 45-60 seconds |

**What you say:** "I built this in 15 minutes. No code. You can too."

**How to deliver it:**
- Show the finished product immediately (the result)
- Then cut to you talking OR voiceover begins
- "15 minutes" and "no code" are the key phrases

### The Script (Full)

```
[HOOK — 0:00-0:03]

"I built this in 15 minutes. No code. You can too."

Let me show you how fast this actually was.

[Sped up screen recording with timestamps]

2:00 — Described what I wanted in plain English
5:00 — Got the first version, asked for tweaks
10:00 — Connected it to [tool] with copy/paste
15:00 — Done. Running automatically.

[Show it working again]

No code. No technical background. Just me describing what I wanted.

[CLOSE — 0:45-0:55]

Believing I could do it before I started.

Your turn.

Want the full recipe? Link in bio.
```

### Filming Instructions

**This is primarily screen recording:**

**Screen Recording Setup:**
- Record at high resolution
- Clean desktop (hide personal stuff)
- Increase font size in ChatGPT so it's readable on mobile
- Use a screen recording tool (Loom, OBS, or built-in)

**Voiceover:**
- Record separately for cleaner audio
- Or record live as you build (more authentic, slightly messier)
- Good mic is critical

**Editing:**
- Speed up the boring parts (2-4x)
- Add timestamps as text overlays
- Keep the interesting moments at normal speed
- Total video should be 45-60 seconds even though the build was 15 minutes

**Energy & Delivery:**
- Voiceover should be conversational, like you're showing a friend
- Point out the key moments: "See? Just described it in plain English."
- Don't over-explain technical stuff
- End with encouragement

**What to Build (Ideas):**
- Meeting notes → action items automation
- Email drafts from voice notes
- Content idea generator
- Simple data formatter
- Anything that saves time and looks impressive

**Pacing Guide:**
- Hook: Fast, show the result immediately
- Body: Mix of sped-up and normal speed
- Timestamps help viewers track progress
- Close: Slow down, land the encouragement

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't show every single step in real time (boring)
- Don't use tools that are too complicated for beginners
- Don't make it look harder than it is
- Don't forget to show the RESULT first (hook them)

## CONTENT PIECE #3: THE PROMPT THAT WORKS

### Overview
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Format** | Carousel (6 slides) |
| **Length** | 6 slides |
| **Funnel Stage** | MOFU (Nurture) |
| **Target Persona** | All |
| **Goal** | Give them a copy-paste framework they can use for anything. |

### The Psychology
People want templates. They want something they can copy, fill in, and use. This gives them the universal prompt structure that works for almost any task.

### Design Direction
- Brand colors: Charcoal background, Cream text, Copper accents
- Code/recipe card aesthetic
- Each slide builds on the previous (the prompt gets longer)
- Consider numbered steps or a "building blocks" visual

### The Slides

**SLIDE 1 (Hook)**
```
Copy this prompt.
Thank me later.

→
```
- Direct, confident
- Creates curiosity

**SLIDE 2**
```
Step 1:

"Act as a [specific role]..."

Example:
"Act as a senior marketing manager 
with 10 years of experience."
```
- Show the template AND an example
- "specific role" in Copper

**SLIDE 3**
```
Step 2:

"I'm trying to accomplish [goal]..."

Example:
"I'm trying to write a cold email 
that gets responses from busy executives."
```
- Building on step 1

**SLIDE 4**
```
Step 3:

"Here's my context: [situation]..."

Example:
"I'm selling B2B software to 
mid-size companies. My target is CTOs."
```
- Context is the secret sauce

**SLIDE 5**
```
Step 4:

"Give me [specific output format]..."

Example:
"Give me 3 versions: 
formal, casual, and direct. 
Keep each under 100 words."
```
- Specific output = better results

**SLIDE 6 (Close)**
```
Full prompt:

"Act as a [role]. 
I'm trying to [goal]. 
Here's my context: [situation]. 
Give me [specific output]."

This works for almost everything.

Save it. Use it. 
@the_concept.kitchen
```
- Full template on one slide
- "almost everything" is the hook
- CTA + handle

### Caption
```

The structure:

2. "I'm trying to accomplish [goal]..."
State your objective clearly.

3. "Here's my context: [situation]..."
Context is the secret sauce. More context = better output.

4. "Give me [specific output format]..."
Tell it exactly how you want the response.

Full template:
"Act as a [role]. I'm trying to [goal]. Here's my context: [situation]. Give me [specific output]."

This works for emails, content, analysis, brainstorming — almost anything.
```

## GENERAL PRODUCTION NOTES FOR PILLAR 3

### Tone Reminders
- This is ACTION content. More energy than Pillars 1-2.
- Keep it practical. Every piece should give them something to DO.
- Don't over-explain. Quick Wins should feel quick.

### Screen Recording Tips
- Increase font size in all apps (readers on mobile)
- Clean desktop (no embarrassing tabs or files)
- Use a tool like Loom, OBS, or QuickTime
- Record at high resolution, export for mobile (9:16)

### When to Use Screen Recording vs. Talking Head
- **Screen recording:** When showing a process or tool
- **Talking head:** When the message is emotional or personal
- **Hybrid:** Best of both worlds for tutorials

### Editing Style for Quick Wins
- Faster cuts than Pillars 1-2
- Speed up boring parts (2-4x)
- Text overlays for key points
- Timestamp overlays help viewers track progress
- Captions are mandatory

### Posting Strategy
- Quick Wins should be 40% of your content (MOFU)
- Balance practical content with emotional content
- Quick Wins perform well on save/bookmark metrics

### What Makes Quick Wins Work
- Specific, actionable, immediate
- "Try this right now" energy
- Show the result first, then the process
- Make them feel capable, not overwhelmed
