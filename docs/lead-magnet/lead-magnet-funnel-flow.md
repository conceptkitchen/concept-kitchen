## STEP 1: THE CONTENT

### Format Options:

**Option A: Talking Head Reel (60-90 sec)**
Best for: Personal connection, algorithm loves it

**Option B: Carousel (5-7 slides)**
Best for: Saves, shares, educational feel

**Option C: Static Post with Strong Hook**
Best for: Quick to create, text-forward

---

### THE POST (Reel Script — Option A)

**Hook (first 2 sec):**
> "AI doesn't work for most people. Here's why."

**Body:**
> "It's not the tool. It's not the prompt. It's how you're asking.
>
> Most people ask vague. They get garbage back. Then they think AI is overhyped.
>
> But there's a skill that changes everything. And nobody teaches it.
>
> It's not prompt engineering. It's communication.
>
> Context. Specifics. Constraints. Examples.
>
> When you learn to ask clearly — AI becomes the most useful tool you've ever touched.
>
> And that same skill? Works on people too. Your team. Your boss. Everyone."

**CTA (last 5 sec):**
> "I made a free guide that breaks this down. Comment RECIPE and I'll send it to you."
