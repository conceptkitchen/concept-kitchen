## THE FULL 20 — RANKED

### #1: "The AI Confidence Audit" (Quiz)
**Score: 9.5/10**

**What it is:** Interactive quiz that diagnoses exactly WHERE they're stuck with AI and gives a personalized first step based on their answers.

**Why it's #1:**
- Interactive = 5-8% opt-in (vs 1-3% for PDFs)
- Personalized results = feels valuable
- Segmentation data = you know who they are
- Low commitment to start = high completion
- Natural upsell: "Based on your results, here's your first recipe..."

**Persona fit:**
- Marcus: "I need to know where to start for interviews"
- Diana: "Finally, something that sees MY situation"
- James: "Quick, tell me exactly what to do"

**Sample questions:**
- "When someone mentions AI at work, you feel: A) Curious B) Anxious C) Skeptical D) Lost"
- "How much time per week can you realistically spend learning? A) 15 min B) 30 min C) 1 hour D) More"
- "Your biggest AI fear is: A) Looking dumb B) Wasting time C) Being replaced D) Missing out"

**Result types:**
- "The Curious Beginner" → Start with X
- "The Overwhelmed Professional" → Start with Y
- "The Skeptical Builder" → Start with Z
