The Framework

Every clear request has four parts:

The Four Elements

1
CONTEXT

Who you are. What's the situation. What you've already tried.

2
SPECIFICS

What exactly you want. Format. Length. Goal.

3
CONSTRAINTS

What to avoid. Tone. Style. Boundaries.

4
EXAMPLE

Show what good looks like. (When helpful.)

That's it. Context. Specifics. Constraints. Example.

But here's the problem: most people forget this in the moment. Or they're too tired to think it through. Or they just want the answer fast.

That's why you don't do the work. You make AI do it.

The Recipe: Make AI Ask YOU

Instead of crafting the perfect prompt, flip it. Give AI this instruction and let it pull the right information out of you.

Copy this into any AI chat:
Before responding to any request I give you: 1. Ask me clarifying questions to understand the full context 2. Keep asking until you have: - My role / who I am - The situation / background - The goal / what I'm trying to achieve - The audience / who this is for - Any constraints / what to avoid 3. Summarize what you understood and ask me to confirm 4. Only after I confirm, give me your response If I'm being vague, push back and ask for specifics. Don't guess.

How to Use It

Option 1: Custom Instructions (ChatGPT)
Go to Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions → Paste it in the "How would you like ChatGPT to respond?" box.

Option 2: Start of Any Conversation
Just paste the instruction at the beginning of a new chat.

Option 3: Attach This Guide
Upload this PDF to your conversation. Tell AI: "Use this as your guide."

What Happens Next

Here's what a conversation looks like when AI asks the right questions:

You: "I need help with an email."
AI: "Sure. A few questions first:
- Who's the email to?
- What's the context — why are you writing?
- What tone do you want?
- How long should it be?
- Anything I should avoid?"
You: "It's to my team. We had a project delay. I want to explain without blaming anyone. Keep it short, no corporate jargon."
AI: "Got it. So you want a short email to your team about the project delay, direct but not blaming, no corporate jargon. Correct?"
You: "Yes."
AI: Delivers something actually useful.

No more garbage outputs. No more "that's not what I meant." No more starting over.

Why This Works

You're not learning a new skill. You're outsourcing the skill to AI.

The framework (Context, Specifics, Constraints, Examples) is how good communicators think. But you don't have to hold it in your head. The instruction makes AI think that way FOR you.

And when AI asks you clarifying questions, it forces YOU to get clear — which makes everything better.
